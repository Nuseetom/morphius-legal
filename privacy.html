Morphius Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how In Good Health Professional Corporation handles information when you use Morphius and its optional QuickBooks Online integration.

Information handled by Morphius

Morphius may process the information you provide in conversations. If you connect QuickBooks Online and request accounting information, Morphius may retrieve the information needed to answer your request, such as company information, reports, or the results of a read-only accounting query.

QuickBooks connection data

Connecting QuickBooks Online uses Intuit's OAuth authorization process. Access and refresh tokens are stored locally in encrypted form so Morphius can maintain the connection. Morphius uses the connection only for authorized, read-only QuickBooks Online requests. It does not create, edit, or delete QuickBooks transactions or records.

Local storage

Conversation requests, responses, and information returned for a request may be retained locally in Morphius conversation records. You can disconnect a QuickBooks Online company through Morphius; disconnecting removes its stored connection credentials.

Third-party services

QuickBooks Online is provided by Intuit. When you authorize the integration or retrieve QuickBooks information, Intuit processes information according to its own privacy practices and terms. This legal-information site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process visitor information under GitHub's policies.

Security

Morphius uses access controls and encrypted storage for QuickBooks connection tokens. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices and questions

You may choose not to connect QuickBooks Online, disconnect a connected company, or contact us with privacy questions or requests at nursetom@msn.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service changes. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
